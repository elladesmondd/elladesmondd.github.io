Big Cats

Here are some pictures and information on big cats!

What are big cats?

Big cats are primarily members of the Panthera genus. They are apex predators known for their size, hunting ability, and most are able to roar. All big cats are carnivores. There are 38 species of cats in the world, but big cats make up some of the most recognizable animals on the planet. Cheetahs are actually their own genus, Aciononyx, because they do not have retractable claws

Fun Facts about big cats

  • Lions are the only true social big cat, living in groups called prides
  • Tigers are the largest cats in the world
  • Leopards are the most adaptable of all big cats
  • Cheetahs are the fastest land animals
