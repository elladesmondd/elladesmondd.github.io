MEET ME!

My name is Ella Desmond. I am a senior Journalism major with a Public Relations concentration at UMass Amherst. I am currently pursuing my Professional Writing and Technical Communication (PWTC) certificate. Through my time at UMass, I have developed proficiency in research, writing, and communication. These skills all work together to make me adept at conveying information efficiently, and making effective recommendations.

I started my journey at UMass as a general Journalism major. I always had an affinity for writing, and loved a research paper - weeks of research and ideas coming together on a page was the most gratifying part of any academic assignment. I believed that journalism would allow me to continue to explore and work with these practices I had become so fond of. After two semesters in the program, I found the major limiting. I loved interviewing experts, gathering data, and "finding the answers," but once a piece was turned in - that was it. I wanted to be able to immerse myself in a project, use what I found to create something larger. This is where I found a real joy in Public Relations. Through PR, I am able to research, analyze, and then turn this work into the "bigger picture" I felt was missing in writng news stories. There was the goal of not just completing a project, but creating change. Through my time at school, I have gained the invaluable skill of being able to understand specific audiences - to not just treat people as a monolith. Through understanding audiences, I am able to make recommendations on what they know they want, and what they might not know yet.

Through my Professional Writing and Technical Communication Certificate, I have gained a proficiency in writing concisely and directly, along with a competency in many softwares I would seldom have had the chance to learn in school. Professional writing allows me to write directly, focusing on ensuring the reader gets the most information in the most efficient time. Technical communication allows me to understand the computer before me, ensuring I can create the content online that I want.
