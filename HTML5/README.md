## What is digital accessibility? :mouse:

Digital accessibility is the practice of ensuring that *everyone*, regardless of their needs or 
abilities, has unilateral access to a webpage or digital content. This means people with, for example, hearing or visual impairments will have the same experience and access to information in digital content, as someone without those impairments.

###  What are title cards? :tv: 
 
Title cards are used in video content to create clear markers throughout a video. 
They can act as:
1. Chapter titles to separate different parts of the video
2. Summariies of key points of information
3. A brief overview of the information to come
   
Title cards improve digital accessibility by clearly **chunking** information in a video.
