What is the project I'm working on? 

For my class, English 381, we have been creating webpages using MadCap Flare. The overarching theme of these websites is digital accessibility and video content, and ultimately how to improve video or visual content by ensuring it is digitally accessible. 

What is digital accessibility? 

Digital accessibility is the practice of ensuring that everyone, regardless of their needs or 
abilities, has unilateral access to a webpage or digital content. This means people with, for example, hearing or visual impairments will have the same experience and access to information in digital content, as someone without those impairments. 

What are title cards? 
 
Title cards are used in video content to create clear markers throughout a video. They can act as chapter titles, summarize key points of information, or give a brief overview of the information to come. Title cards improve digital accessibility by clearly chunking information in a video.
